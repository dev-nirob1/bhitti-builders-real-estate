How we work

From first call to final handover

A clear five-step process, so you always know what is happening and what comes next.

  1. 1

    Consultation

    We listen to your plot, budget and goals, then outline what is possible.

  2. 2

    Design

    Architects and engineers prepare plans, drawings and a clear cost estimate.

  3. 3

    Approval

    We handle permits and authority approvals so you never have to chase paperwork.

  4. 4

    Construction

    Skilled crews build to plan, with regular site updates and quality checks.

  5. 5

    Handover

    Final inspection, documents and keys, followed by warranty support.
