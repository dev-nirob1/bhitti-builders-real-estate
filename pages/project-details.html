About the project

Forty-eight homes on a tight Dhanmondi plot, delivered two weeks ahead of schedule.

Bhitti Heights is an eight-storey apartment building in one of Dhaka's most established neighbourhoods. Every home has cross ventilation, generous balconies and a layout drawn around how families actually live.

The building sits a short walk from Dhanmondi Lake, good schools and hospitals, with a covered car park on the ground floor and a landscaped rooftop for residents.
