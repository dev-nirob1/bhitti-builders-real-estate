About the project

Forty-eight homes on a tight Dhanmondi plot, delivered two weeks ahead of schedule.

Bhitti Heights is an eight-storey apartment building in one of Dhaka's most established neighbourhoods. Every home has cross ventilation, generous balconies and a layout drawn around how families actually live.

The building sits a short walk from Dhanmondi Lake, good schools and hospitals, with a covered car park on the ground floor and a landscaped rooftop for residents.

Behind the build

  1. A tight plot

    The plot sat on a narrow lane with homes on three sides and room for one delivery truck at a time. A high water table and a firm handover date left no room for a standard construction sequence.

  2. How we built it

    Deliveries ran in timed windows, concrete and steel moved at night and drainage was finished before the superstructure so the monsoon could not stop work. Neighbours got a weekly note and a named site engineer to call.

  3. What we delivered

    All forty-eight homes were handed over two weeks early and the building passed its final inspection first time. Many of the first owners have since recommended us to family and friends.
