Our story

It started with one house in Dhanmondi

In 2009 a few of us left salaried jobs to build a single family home. The owner came to the site every evening and asked a hundred questions. We answered every one. That habit never left.

We learned early that most problems in construction are not technical. They are conversations that never happened: about cost, about the schedule, about how the finished building should feel. So we put those conversations first.

Since then we have built more than a hundred buildings across Dhaka, from small apartment blocks to commercial towers. The owner still visits. We still answer.
