Residential construction

Apartments, duplexes and townhouses built for families who plan to stay.

Overview

A home is the biggest purchase most families will ever make, so we treat the process with the care it deserves. From the first sketch to the day you receive your keys, one team stays responsible for your project and explains every decision in plain language.

We build apartment blocks, duplexes and standalone homes on your plot or ours. Every project follows the same routine: a clear written scope, a realistic schedule, weekly progress updates and a final inspection you are invited to attend.

What is included

  • Design and planning

    Architectural layouts, structural design and a detailed cost estimate before any work begins.

  • Permits and approvals

    We prepare the drawings, submit them to the authorities and follow up until approval.

  • Construction

    Foundation, structure, masonry and all building work by our own supervised crews.

  • Finishes and fittings

    Flooring, tiling, paint, doors, windows and sanitary fittings to the agreed specification.

  • Utilities

    Electricity, gas and water connections arranged and tested before handover.

  • Handover and warranty

    Final inspection, documents, keys and a written warranty with a team ready after you move in.
